START OFFLINE
1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. Open index.html inside the extracted presentation folder.
3. Choose Full screen and present with the arrow keys.
No installation, server or internet connection is required. Keep the scripts,
stylesheets and assets folder beside index.html. Use a modern desktop browser.

PRESENTATION CONTROLS
Right / Left arrow or Page Down / Page Up: next / previous slide
Space: next slide
Home / End: first / last slide
F: full screen
O: slide navigator
G: source image gallery
N: presenter notes
R: source register and drawing audit
M: motion on / off
B: build plan observations one at a time (on annotated plans)
J: animate the route categories in sequence (on circulation slides)
Escape: close a dialog or leave full screen
Auto play: advance every 20 seconds, pausing while a dialog is open

PLAN ANALYSIS
Build analysis reveals one written observation at a time. Show all restores
all observations. Click a callout to emphasize its line and plan anchor.
Leaders on / off controls the analytical lines.
Inspect original + zoom opens the full source drawing and department index.
Click a numbered department to zoom into its source location. Reset restores
all the drawing. Guided tour visits the named departments automatically.
Wheel to zoom and drag to pan inside enlarged figure viewers.
Read source sheet opens the corresponding original study page offline.

CIRCULATION AND BUBBLES
Click a category to isolate it. Animate journeys cycles through the categories.
Tata: red main hall, thicker blue secondary hall, dotted blue room-entry branch.
MVR: red patient / visitor and purple shared staff / service traces.
The service filter recolors the combined staff / service trace for readability;
it does not establish a second, separate corridor.
Tata bubble positions, sizes, colors and relationships follow the supplied PDF.
Labels are typeset clearly in Speda; the untouched source page remains linked.
MVR's new bubble diagram is a functional relationship schematic, not a plan.
On the comparison board, choose a topic for an animated, readable close-up.

SOURCE METHOD
Walls, rooms and source zoning colors are preserved. Colors may differ between
drawings; written place names remain the primary key. The new leader lines
identify locations and explain relationships; they are not zone boundaries.
Original PDF vector paths provide the team circulation geometry. Invisible
PDF shapes are omitted. New site / BMT approach paths follow visible passages
and named destinations as architectural interpretations. Shared segments are
deliberate. Animation direction does not specify traffic regulations.
The original source resolution limits very small labels.
See SOURCE-AUDIT.txt for conflicts, source mappings and the department index.
